GUIDE partnership

Bring GUIDE dementia care to your patients, with AbleLiving behind you.

GUIDE is a CMS program (July 2024 to June 2032) that pays a monthly amount for each patient with dementia to cover coordinated care, caregiver support, and respite. AbleLiving holds the CMS agreement. Your practice delivers the hands-on care; we handle the rest.

Your practice ✓ Refer eligible patients
✓ Comprehensive assessments: initial, reassessment, annual
✓ Care navigator check-ins and monitoring
✓ Home safety assessment and community referrals
AbleLiving ✓ Intake, consents, charts, and CMS enrollment
✓ Clinical sign-off by our Advanced Practitioners
✓ Care plan, 24/7 line, medications, respite
✓ Caregiver education, coding, billing, and payment
How it works
1 · ReferSend the referral form
2 · EnrollWe verify, consent, and chart
3 · AssessYour clinician visits the home
4 · Sign offOur provider verifies and signs
5 · Check inOngoing visits by tier
6 · Get paidMonthly via Bill.com
Who qualifies ✓ Dementia diagnosis, any stage
✓ Original Medicare Parts A and B, Medicare primary
✓ Not in Medicare Advantage, PACE, or hospice
✓ Not in long-term nursing home care Nine services, one care team Assessment · Care plan · Monitoring · Medications · 24/7 line · Care transitions · Community referrals · Caregiver training · Respite (up to $2,625 per year)
