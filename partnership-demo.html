Part 2 · The care team

Your team on the front line, the AbleLiving Clinical Team overseeing.

CMS requires a care navigator and a clinician with dementia expertise on every GUIDE care team. The AbleLiving Clinical Team supervises the team and signs off on every comprehensive assessment. In the Hybrid model, your practice fills both roles.
